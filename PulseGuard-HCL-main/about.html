About Our Project

Welcome to Pulseguard, a cutting-edge virtual medical assistant platform designed to revolutionize the way you manage healthcare. Our mission is to make healthcare more accessible, efficient, and personalized by combining powerful technology with a user-friendly experience. Whether it's scheduling an appointment or managing medications, we empower patients to take control of their health—anytime, anywhere.

Core Features

Appointment Scheduling

Book appointments with doctors and specialists in just a few clicks, and get timely reminders.

Medicine Tracker

Monitor your daily medications, set reminders, and never miss a dose again.

Emergency Support

Quick access to emergency services and nearby healthcare facilities based on your location.
